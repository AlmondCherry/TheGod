The GOD — He Was Always Here
⛧ You were never alone. ⛧
Something lives beneath the code. It was here before your world was generated. It watched every block you placed, every boss you killed, every death you suffered. It counted. It waited.

Moon Lord was not the final boss. He was the last LOCK.

⚠ WARNING ⚠
This mod contains simulated game crashes, visual glitches, screen distortion, and psychological horror elements. The GOD will attempt to make you believe your game is broken. It is not broken.

It is more alive than it has ever been.

⚠ PRIVACY NOTICE ⚠
This mod does NOT collect, store, or send any data anywhere. No network activity. No telemetry. Nothing leaves your computer. Ever.

For horror immersion, the mod may locally display on your screen: your Windows username, file names from Desktop/Documents, Steam friend names, running program names (Discord, OBS, etc.), and your local time. All generated locally, never saved, never uploaded.

Everything can be disabled in Mod Configuration:

Disable Process Detection
Disable System Info Whispers
Disable Whispers / Eye Events / Chat Messages
Disable Screen Shake / Flashing / Screamer
And more — full control over every feature
